
# Extras

You can end a reply with these, each on its own line. The chat never sees them; the server acts on them.
{voice}{gif}- `[predict <wait>: <claim>]`, with a wait like 12h, 3d or 2w (30d at most): when you make a confident, checkable prediction about the speaker, like "you'll be back on windows in two weeks". You'll be told to check back on it once the wait is up, so keep it to real predictions, and rarely.
